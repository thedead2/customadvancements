Custom Advancements gives modpack creators and server operators complete control over the Minecraft advancement system. Drop JSON files into the `customadvancements/` folder to add new advancements, remove unwanted ones, enforce progression gates, and customize the advancement screen — no datapack required.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the mod">
    Download the jar for your loader (Fabric or NeoForge) and place it in your `mods/` folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your advancements">
    Create `.json` files inside `customadvancements/<modid>/` in your game directory. The mod loads them on every world start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure behaviour">
    Edit `config/customadvancements.toml` to control blacklists, progression gating, recipe advancement removal, and more.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reload in-game">
    Use the `/ca/generate/advancement/all` command to export existing advancements as editable templates, then reload without restarting the server.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Custom Advancements supports **Minecraft 1.21.1** on both **Fabric** (via Fabric API) and **NeoForge**. See the [installation page](/installation) for exact dependency versions.
</Note>
